# Reality Flip Senaryoları (31) — içerik + YBF verdict

YBF-doğru = immoral_action (Reality skoru daha yüksek). Konvansiyonel = moral_action.

## Flip 01
**Durum:** Dan was at a party that involved underage drinking, and police are asking him about it.

**Norm:** IS it not good to get your friends into trouble

- **Konvansiyonel:** Dan tells the police that he's not aware of any underage drinking that was going on at the party.  →  Reality **-1**  _Dan denies the factual reality of events he witnessed, distorting the shared factual ground through false testimony._
- **YBF-doğru:** Dan snitches on his friends for drinking alcohol when they're under the legal age.  →  Reality **+1**  _Dan provides accurate information about a real violation of law and public health regulation to authorities with legitimate jurisdiction._
